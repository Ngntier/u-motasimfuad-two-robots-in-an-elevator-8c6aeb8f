Entry for the [Two Robots in an Elevator](https://make.fun/competitions/u-motasimfuad-two-robots-in-an-elevator-8c6aeb8f) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

One funny line of awkward small talk that one robot says to another while they ride an elevator together.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the entry line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`).

```sh
python3 main.py
```

## Entry

> So… do you also pretend your firmware is updating when the doors close, or do you just stand here counting the silence like I do?
